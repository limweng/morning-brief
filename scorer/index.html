BOB 2026 Judging Scorer

Upload team submissions (PDF or PowerPoint). Each file is scored on the five CA Entry criteria and ranked.

1. Add project files

Drop PDF or PPTX files here or click to browse. One file per project. You can add several at once.

2. Scoring engine

With a key, Claude Opus 5.5 reads each deck and scores it against the criteria with reasons. Without a key, the page gives a quick keyword-based estimate. Either way, you can adjust any score by hand.

3. Scores

1 to 4 Does not meet 5 Meets expectation 6 to 7 Exceeds 8 to 10 Outstanding

Team Score = sum of (score x weightage) x 10, out of 100. Same formula as the judging spreadsheet.
